Why 90 days, and what we refuse to promise

Expecting financial transformation in 90 days is a measurement error. The output of 90 days is learning and infrastructure. Financial results compound over one to two years.

So we do not sell you a 3.7x ROI slide. We close three gaps you can verify yourself: a maturity gap, a leadership gap and a governance gap. Visibility established. Capability levers running. Charter and agent registry in force.
